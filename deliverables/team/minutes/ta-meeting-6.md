## Presentation structure

Salman shared feedback he has given other teams:

- Open with context, not only the problem. For example: the share of developers using Godot, its rising usage, and the gap in AMD's GPU compute pipeline.
- Include a slide with a partner quote that states the core pain point the project solves.
- Keep all user stories on a background slide, for reference during Q&A.
- The demo comes after the background section and must fit inside the 20 minutes.
  - Pre-record or pre-simulate where possible instead of running everything live.
  - Show the top user flow. Other flows can come up in Q&A.
- Technical diagrams should be as detailed as possible, while the verbal explanation stays high level.
  - Use sequence-flow diagrams alongside architecture diagrams.
  - Consider splitting the technical section by subgroup.
- The learnings slide should list both process learnings and technical or tool-specific ones. Highlight the top one or two verbally, but list all of them on the slide.
- Add a handoff slide covering how the product will be transferred to the partner.
